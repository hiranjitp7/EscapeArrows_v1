Privacy Policy — Escape Arrows!

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Orbitfox Studio (“we” or “us”) publishes Escape Arrows! (“the app”). This policy explains what information the app stores and how its advertising and purchase services handle information.

Information stored by the game

The app does not require an account. On Android, game progress, settings, hints, undo charges, daily challenge information, and collected artwork are stored locally in the app’s storage on your device. The browser version stores equivalent game information in that browser’s local storage. Orbitfox Studio does not receive this game profile on a game server.

Advertising

The Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google’s SDK may collect and share the device’s IP address, ad and app interactions (such as taps and video views), diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID. IP addresses may be used to estimate a device’s general location. Google may use this information for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform presents privacy and consent choices. Available choices can be reopened in the app under Settings → Privacy Settings. You can also manage advertising settings in your Android device settings. Google explains its handling in its Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure .

Purchases

The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. The app checks purchase status so it can enable or restore the purchase. Orbitfox Studio does not collect or store payment-card details. Google handles transaction information under its own policies.

Sharing

The game profile stored on your device is not uploaded to Orbitfox Studio. Information handled by the advertising SDK is shared with Google as described above. Google Play processes purchase transactions.

Security, retention, and deletion

Game information stays in the app’s private storage on your device. You can remove it by clearing Escape Arrows!’ app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. In a browser, clear the browser’s site data to remove the locally stored game information. The app does not keep a server-side copy of this game profile.

Google handles advertising-related information and purchase records through its services and applies its own security, retention, and deletion practices. Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Orbitfox Studio at orbitfoxstudio@gmail.com.
