# Privacy Policy — Escape Arrows!

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

Escape Arrows! does not require an account. Campaign progress, preferences, hints, undo charges, daily streak information, and collected artwork are stored locally on the device using Android Preferences. In a web browser, equivalent settings are stored in that browser's local storage.

The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data to select, measure, and protect ads according to its privacy policy. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices before ad requests are made. Players can reopen available privacy choices from Settings.

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. Purchase and restore status is checked through the app's billing plugin. Sound effects are generated on-device; vibration uses the device haptics service where available.
